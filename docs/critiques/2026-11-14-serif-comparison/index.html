VD-96 (DR-03): a text serif for prose

The design review of 8 October found every voice on the site in the same condensed sans, with condensed capitals at every heading level, and prose - ledes, notes, where a figure comes from - with no voice of its own. It proposed a roman text serif for that prose, condensed capitals for the h1 only, and h2 and below in the words as written. This page sets the two candidates it named beside the site as built, on three real pages, so that one can be chosen before anything is built.

What you are looking at

  • Current: the page as lapledger.org serves it before the app takes over, from a build of main at 7d60b10.
  • Source Serif 4 and Newsreader: the same page with serif.css laid over it. Each column changes the prose face and the heading case and nothing else: the sizes, weights, colours and text widths are the site's own tokens, untouched. At the same size Newsreader sets visibly smaller than Source Serif 4 and Saira; if it is chosen, the build gives its roles a size of their own rather than reading this page as the size it would ship at.
  • In serif: the lede, a note under a heading or a block, a note box, a table's foot, a figure's note, the How it is derived text, a source line, the reading of a recorded disagreement, and the running prose on /data. In Saira still: tiles, tables, labels, pills, photo captions and credits - the label register, which D-62 settled.
  • The page title stays in condensed capitals. Section heads drop the capitals; Section heads below puts them back, to see the face on its own.
  • These are copies: the links do nothing, and the race and driver pages show their opening state, before the app adds what it adds. The race page loads its photographs from Wikimedia Commons.

To decide

Which one: Source Serif 4, Newsreader, or neither (prose stays in Saira, and DR-03 is declined). And, separately: section heads in the words as written, as DR-03 proposes, or kept in capitals. Nothing is built until the item records the choice; the build then sets the chosen face as a token and a font role in tokens.css, writes it into design-system.md section 2 Type, and updates the type tests.
